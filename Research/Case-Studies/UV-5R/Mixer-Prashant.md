# Mixer Research for UV-5R
The UV-5R does its mixing inside the RDA1846 chip inside of the radio, which has an internal DSP which processes the signal. The chip has a VCO (Voltage Controlled Oscillator), which is modulated with the voice signal for the mic within the DSP unit.
